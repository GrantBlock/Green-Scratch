How the whole system flows, and where you start and end

Two loops run through one system. In the first you publish a document and visitors see it. In the second a visitor reaches out and you work the lead to a close. The Vault is your side, the Hub is the public side, and Apply joins them.

You act by hand Happens automatically or the visitor acts
Loop 1 · Outbound

You publish a document, visitors find it

Loop 2 · Inbound

A visitor reaches out, you work the lead to a close

Your start and end, in one place

Day to day, you start at the Vault login

Open Log List first. New rows are inbound leads. Open Releases when you have a document to publish.

Day to day, you end at one of two places

A lead reaches Closed and goes to Archive, or a document reaches Apply and appears in Grab this.

Revisions after publishing

Log them by hand in Change Management. A new file against an existing entry keeps the same serial, and Apply publishes it again.
