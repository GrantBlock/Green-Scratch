# Session V3: Calendar, Money, Knowledge, Assistant and settings

Address: `vault.blockconceptsllc.com`.

Design:
1. **Calendar:** week and agenda views; event kinds (customer visit, supplier visit, site measurement, installation, networking event, trade show, call); trips; add-event sheet.
2. **Money:** receivables (invoice, customer, due date, days overdue, amount, Draft reminder), milestone list, invoice composer (review before send), expense entry with receipt photo and category dropdown.
3. **Knowledge:** list by kind (application research, sales research, article, trip plan, note), Markdown item view, trip itinerary.
4. **Assistant panel:** side panel on desktop, full-height sheet on phone; citations, proposed actions with Confirm and Cancel, monthly spend meter, role chips (Quoting, Building, Supporting).
5. **Library (Vault) and Claude section:** templates; company, customer and project configuration, glossary and standard works with a Markdown editor and version history, and a banner that these are instructions for the assistant.
6. **Settings:** categories, templates, assistant cap, agent email, publishing to the Hub, backups and export.

Out of scope this round: Books screens (ledger, reconciliation, statements).
Data: Event, Trip, Milestone, Invoice, Expense, Knowledge item, Template.
