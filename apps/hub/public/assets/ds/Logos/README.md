Block Concepts mark files. The master `block-concepts-mark.svg` is single-ink `bc-mark-print` (#2F5D80) and is for light grounds and Word documents; `block-concepts-mark.png` is the same mark as a 2000px transparent PNG for places that cannot take SVG, such as the Word cover. `block-concepts-mark-dark.svg` is the master with its ink changed to `bc-steel-blue` (#4682B4) for the dark website, because the master reads 2.79:1 on the near-black ground. `block-concepts-mark-reversed.svg` is the master in white (#FFFFFF) for Steel Blue panels and dark photos. Show each only through `<img>`; the ink cannot be changed from CSS.
